RankupRL

A local-first desktop companion for Rocket League players who want to improve. Live MMR tracking, watchlists, and study notes from coaching content — all on your own machine.

Private alpha · in active development

What it does

Tracker

Live per-playlist MMR, tier, and lifetime stats pulled from tracker.gg for the Rocket League account you connect.

Watchlists

Group friends, rivals, and pros to track. Star any player to anchor your Account view to their stats.

Coaching library (soon)

Ingest videos from YouTube / IG / TikTok, transcribe, and organize tips into searchable study notes.

Local-first

Your data lives in a SQLite file on your machine. No account, no remote backend, no telemetry. Optional Google Drive backup ships in a future phase.

How accounts work

RankupRL uses Sign in with Steam and Sign in with Epic Games as the canonical way to identify your Rocket League account. Each provider's OAuth flow runs in your real browser; the access token lands in your local database and is used only to call that provider's APIs as you. Nothing is sent to any RankupRL-controlled server — there isn't one yet.

See the privacy policy for the full data-handling story.

Status

RankupRL is a personal project in private alpha. The desktop binary is not yet publicly distributed. This page exists as the public landing required by OAuth providers (Epic Account Services, Steam Web API) and to document the privacy policy.
